What Does Air-Gapped Mean?

An air-gapped system is physically and digitally isolated from the public internet and external networks. Because my local Gemini Carbon 4 instance operates entirely offline on a secure local machine, student data, curriculum assets, and clinical interactions never touch external cloud servers, guaranteeing absolute FERPA compliance and ironclad data privacy.

Through this architecture, I achieved 100% WCAG 2.2 AA compliance (9:1 contrast), strict adherence to ADA Title II standards, zero textbook costs ($0 ZTC) for students, and completely private, local AI operations.

Why U.S. DOL ETA Advisory TEN No. 07-25 (Feb 13, 2026) Matters for Community Colleges

Voluntary Federal Framework (5 + 7)

Unlike statutory civil-rights mandates such as ADA Title II or FERPA, U.S. Department of Labor (DOL) Employment and Training Administration (ETA) Training and Employment Notice (TEN) No. 07-25 (Artificial Intelligence Literacy Framework, issued February 13, 2026) is a voluntary federal workforce and community college framework. Almost no one in higher education has translated this federal advisory into live course architecture yet. Across the AWC EXW fleet, we proactively operationalize all 5 Foundational Content Areas (Understand AI Principles, Explore AI Uses, Direct AI Effectively, Evaluate Outputs, Use AI Responsibly) and all 7 Community College Delivery Design Principles (Experiential Learning, Embedded Context, Complementary Human Judgment, $0 ZTC Equitable Access, Pathways for Continued Learning, Enabling Faculty Roles, and Agile Curriculum-as-Code Design) inside Simon Sinek’s Golden Circle (WHY → HOW → WHAT).
