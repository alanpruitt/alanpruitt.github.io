What Does Air-Gapped Mean?

An air-gapped system is physically and digitally isolated from the public internet and external networks. Because my local Gemini Argon 4 instance operates entirely offline on a secure local machine, student data, curriculum assets, and clinical interactions never touch external cloud servers, guaranteeing absolute FERPA compliance and ironclad data privacy.

Through this architecture, I achieved 100% WCAG 2.2 AA compliance (9:1 contrast), strict adherence to ADA Title II standards, zero textbook costs ($0 ZTC) for students, and completely private, local AI operations.
