Expandable Implementation Guides (Select Any Row to Open)

Rule 1: Never Grade Prompt Polish—Grade Primary Rule Verification

Students paying $20/month for frontier reasoning models will always generate smoother prose than peers using a shared library Chromebook. Level the playing field by grading whether the student accurately identified the Pattern, cited the exact Course SSoT Rule, and verified the Solve against local telemetry.

Rule 2: Protect Multilingual & Borderland Voices (EN / Español Parity)

Explicitly permit students to brainstorm or verify concepts in Spanish or English while requiring transparent disclosure of AI translation/editing support—and never penalize authentic regional syntax using automated "AI probability" scores.

Rule 3: The Triple-Hash (###) Student Prompt Isolation Habit

Teach workforce-ready prompt hygiene (U.S. DOL TEN No. 07-25 Area 3 & Area 5): wrap anonymized case data inside ###CASE_DATA_START### ... ###CASE_DATA_END### delimiters and strip all names, student IDs, and clinical identifiers before touching any external tool.

Five Turnkey 10-Minute Department & In-Service Micro-Labs

Designed for division chairs, faculty leads, and Teaching & Learning Centers: each micro-lab fits into a 10-minute agenda slot (5 minutes solo + 5 minutes pair/table debrief) with zero prep slides required.

Lab 1 • 10 Min

The Monday-Morning Vulnerability Swap

Solo (5 min): Pick one current assignment where pasted AI prose could earn a "B" today. Circle the single learning outcome you actually care about measuring.

Debrief (5 min): Trade with a colleague and add one local data anchor, simulator metric, or 2-sentence oral check that AI cannot fabricate.

Lab 2 • 10 Min

One-Sentence Traffic-Light Syllabus Rule

Solo (5 min): Draft three crisp 1-sentence rules for your syllabus: one Red (human baseline), one Amber (scoped thought-partner), and one Green (AI output audit).

Debrief (5 min): Read your sentences aloud—if a working first-generation student at 11:00 PM cannot tell what is allowed in 10 seconds, cut the jargon in half.

Lab 3 • 10 Min

Product vs. Process Grade-Weight Audit

Solo (5 min): Write down your last major project's point breakdown: what % rewarded polished final prose vs. observable reasoning steps (Pattern, SSoT Rule, Revision Diff)?

Debrief (5 min): Shift at least 40% of the rubric weight onto verifiable process & rule accuracy without increasing your grading time.

Lab 4 • 10 Min

HSI Paywall, Mobile & FERPA Barrier Check

Solo (5 min): Test one AI-involved activity against three checks: (1) Can it be completed at $0? (2) Does it work on a smartphone? (3) Does it require zero student PII?

Debrief (5 min): Replace any paid or login-gated step with an instructor-supplied sample output or $0 ZTC open web tool.

Lab 5 • 10 Min

The 4-Question Metacognitive Exam Wrapper

Solo (5 min): Attach 4 post-quiz questions worth 5% recovery credit: (1) How I prepared, (2) Error pattern (concept vs. rule misread), (3) Exact SSoT page/rule fix, (4) Next-unit adjustment.

Debrief (5 min): Share how error-pattern diagnosis builds self-regulated learners faster than re-lecturing.
