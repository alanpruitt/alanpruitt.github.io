## The Pattern
Federal and state workforce development agencies face a critical execution gap between high-level federal policy directives—specifically the U.S. Department of Labor (DOL) Employment and Training Administration (ETA) Training and Employment Notice (TEN) No. 07-25 (*Artificial Intelligence Literacy Framework*, issued February 13, 2026)—and front-line curriculum deployment in community colleges, Hispanic-Serving Institutions (HSIs), and regional workforce boards.

## The Rule
U.S. DOL ETA Advisory TEN No. 07-25 establishes **5 Foundational AI Content Areas** (`1. Understand AI Principles`, `2. Explore AI Uses`, `3. Direct AI Effectively`, `4. Evaluate Outputs`, `5. Use AI Responsibly`) alongside **7 Community College Delivery Design Principles** (`1. Enable Experiential Learning`, `2. Embed Learning in Context`, `3. Build Complementary Human Skills`, `4. Address Prerequisites & Equitable Access`, `5. Create Pathways for Continued Learning`, `6. Prepare Enabling Roles`, `7. Design for Agility`). Simultaneously, federal ADA Title II (`28 CFR Part 35`) and Section 508 mandates require every digital learning artifact to meet verifiable accessibility standards without commercial vendor lock-in or student PII exposure.

## The Solve
By pairing a Git-governed **Curriculum-as-Code (`S-A-R`: Scalable / Auditable / Repeatable)** pipeline with air-gapped Edge AI inference nodes (`Google Gemma Argon 4` and next-generation `Carbon 4`), automated **WCAG 2.2 AA (`9:1` Double-Contrast Guard)** and **WCAG 3.0 cognitive accessibility continuous integration**, and the **Clinical Framing Protocol (`Pattern / Rule / Solve`)**, **Webcognita LLC (SDVOSB)** serves as a **Non-Competing 50-State Enabling Resource** for **NSF TechAccess: AI-Ready America** lead applicants—translating federal workforce AI literacy mandates directly into auditable, zero-cost OER and **K–16 Dual-Enrollment Deliverables**.
