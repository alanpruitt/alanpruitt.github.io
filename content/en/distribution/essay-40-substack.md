When the U.S. Department of Labor (DOL) Employment and Training Administration (ETA) issued **Training and Employment Notice (TEN) No. 07-25** (*Artificial Intelligence Literacy Framework*, February 13, 2026), most higher education and workforce programs treated it like another static policy PDF.

A conventional compliance response would have been simple: paste a two-sentence "AI Policy" paragraph into a syllabus, link a slide deck on prompt writing, and check the administrative box.

Every time I encounter a federal workforce standard, however, I test my own engineering against the same counterfactual question:

**What if I didn't operationalize the framework?**

What if I left **U.S. DOL ETA Advisory TEN No. 07-25** trapped inside a policy memo—while the first-generation community college students I teach along the **Yuma, AZ / Imperial Valley, CA / San Luis Río Colorado** borderland graduated into an AI-reshaped clinical and allied-health workforce knowing only how to copy-paste unverified chatbot outputs?

Policy memos do not train problem framers. Executable architecture does.

### Engineering the Crosswalk: TEN No. 07-25 as Executable Code

Drawing on my experience as an **Adjunct Faculty member of Exercise Science & Wellness at Arizona Western College**, a pro bono **Generative AI Strategist at The University of Arizona (School of Nutritional Sciences and Wellness — Yuma Campus)**, and **Founder & Principal Consultant of Webcognita LLC (VOSB)**, I independently engineered my **EXW Master Library (`S-A-R v2.2`: Scalable / Auditable / Repeatable)** to compile **TEN No. 07-25** directly into every module, rubric, simulator, and Git pre-commit gate across my **$0 ZTC Open Educational Resource (`OER`)** course fleet (`EXW 101`, `EXW 150`, `EXW 210`, `EXW 245`, and `EXW 265`):

1. **Content Area 1 — Understand AI Principles (`The Centaur Protocol`):** I frame every student as the **Clinical Architect / Pilot** and AI strictly as a probabilistic calculator requiring human verification via **`Pattern / Rule / Solve`**.
2. **Content Area 2 — Explore AI Uses (`Zero-Login Edge Simulators`):** Students run authentic cases on my open-access clinical web apps (`exwtoolkit.com` 9-Box Biometrics & Live NWS `KNYL` Yuma Heat-Stress Engine and `paga2018.dev`) with zero logins, paywalls, or tracking cookies.
3. **Content Area 3 — Direct AI Effectively (`Part 1: P-T-C-F Prompt Engineering`):** Students construct structured **Persona, Task, Context, and Format (`P-T-C-F`)** prompts wrapped in strict triple-hash (`###`) delimiters.
