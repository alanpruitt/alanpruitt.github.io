What if I didn't automate the threshold?
(Essay 39 is live at alanpruitt [.] com)

When a default LMS rubric assigns 1 baseline point to an unsubmitted or clinically unsafe criterion instead of 0, two systems fail quietly at the same time.

First, unearned points inflate early mastery grades, hiding safety errors against federal benchmarks like PAGA 2018, the 2025-2030 Dietary Guidelines, and ACSM 12th Edition.

Second, early-alert student success telemetry cannot distinguish between a student who attempted the lab and a student who never opened it.

Manually clicking through dozens of rubrics across 11 Canvas shells to check minimum point floors does not scale.

So I encoded rubric threshold validation directly into my Curriculum-as-Code (S-A-R Standard: Scalable, Auditable, Repeatable) pipeline:

1. GraphQL & REST Rubric Floor Auditing
My Python inspection scripts scan every assignment rubric across EXW 101, 150, 210, 245, and 265, flag any non-zero minimum rating floor, and patch criteria from 1 pt to 0 pts behind mandatory backup and DRY_RUN verification gates.

2. Zero-Floor Recalibration with Unlimited Attempts
On my 10-7-0 Mastery Grading Scale, 0/10 (Recalibrate) is never punitive. Every lab pairs a clean zero floor with unlimited resubmissions and bilingual WCAG 3.0 feedback (In Plain English and Norteno-Spanish) using Pattern / Rule / Solve.

3. Air-Gapped Apple Silicon Edge AI
Instead of sending gradebook telemetry to cloud chatbots, my M1/M2 workstations run local Gemini Carbon 4 models via Ollama and the Antigravity IDE to audit 9:1 ADA Title II contrast and log W3C PROV-O receipts with 100% FERPA sovereignty.
