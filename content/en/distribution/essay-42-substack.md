### Defining Compliance as Code

Drawing on my work as an **Adjunct Faculty member of Exercise Science & Wellness at Arizona Western College**, a **Generative AI Strategist at The University of Arizona**, and **Principal of Webcognita LLC (VOSB)**, I formalized a third pillar alongside **Curriculum as Code (CaC)** and **Curriculum as a Service (CaaS)** inside my **S-A-R Standard (Scalable / Auditable / Repeatable)**:

> **Compliance as Code:** The deterministic encoding of regulatory, accessibility, privacy, and clinical safety mandates—specifically **ADA Title II (April 24, 2026 deadline)**, **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** (enforced at my **9:1 Double-Contrast Guard**), **WCAG 3.0 APCA & Cognitive Plain-Language**, **FERPA Zero-PII**, and **Federal Clinical Single Sources of Truth** (*PAGA 2018*, *2025–2030 DGA*, *ACSM 12th Ed.*, and *NASM OPT*)—directly into automated Git pre-commit linters, CI/CD pipelines, and real-time upstream breaking-change monitors.

Instead of auditing courses by hand after publication, **Compliance as Code** makes it structurally impossible to deploy a curriculum page that violates contrast thresholds, skips a semantic heading level (`h2 -> h3`), leaks student PII, or drifts from federal clinical guidelines.

### Inside the 6-Month Master Research Log Observatory (92 Dated Entries)

Between **May 30, 2026 and November 2026**, my 48-hour research telemetry pipeline accumulated **92 chronological intelligence briefs** (`4,500+` lines of structured Markdown) tracking Google Workspace, Instructure Canvas, Executive Order 14434 Super Intelligence (`SI`), and peer-reviewed Curriculum-as-Code literature.

Every 48 hours, when I append a new intelligence brief and run `make sync-research-log`:

1. **Deterministic Normalization & Sanitization:** My Python ingestion pipeline validates semantic heading hierarchy, strips decorative Unicode icons for screen-reader clarity, and writes the canonical Markdown source to `00_GOVERNANCE/MASTER_RESEARCH_LOG_CAC_CAAS.md`.
2. **Incremental SHA-256 `DOM GraphRAG` Indexing:** The pipeline hashes every dated entry, reuses cached embeddings for all unchanged entries in **1.7 seconds**, and embeds only the newly added 48-hour delta into my **3,264-vector `DOM GraphRAG` index** via local `nomic-embed-text`.
3. **48-Hour Breaking-Change Alerting:** If a newly ingested entry flags a high-severity change in **Canvas GraphQL (`/api/graphql`)**, **New Quizzes**, **LTI 1.3**, or **WCAG 2.2 AA / 3.0 APCA**, the script automatically dispatches a **`[CRITICAL] EXW-MASTER-LIBRARY Engineering Alert`** to my institutional inbox with concrete file-level remediation steps—weeks before an upstream LMS deploy can impact my students.
4. **Automated Observatory & Compendium Rebuild:** Finally, the pipeline regenerates `alanpruitt.com/research-log/`, compiles **Compendium 07 PDF**, and updates `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt`.

### Slow is Smooth, Smooth is Fast

*What if I didn't log the telemetry?*

Without six months of structured upstream intelligence and executable **Compliance as Code** gates, even a well-designed digital course fleet eventually succumbs to API rot and regulatory drift.
