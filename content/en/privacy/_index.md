## Data Sovereignty & Privacy Principles

This platform is engineered under the principle of minimal data collection. We do not monetize personal data, deploy invasive tracking mechanisms, or sell user information.

---

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect

* **No Analytics Trackers:** We do not use third-party behavioral analytics, heatmaps, or advertising trackers.
* **No Tracking Cookies:** This website operates without session tracking or persistent advertising cookies.

### 2. Information We Process

* **Server Logs:** Like all web servers, technical connection data (including IP addresses, user agent headers, and request timestamps) is processed by our hosting infrastructure solely to maintain site stability, performance, and security.
* **Direct Communications:** When you initiate an email inquiry or request an institutional strategy briefing, your email address and message contents are used exclusively to respond to your request.

### 3. Third-Party Integrations

External links to companion sites (such as `webcognita.com`) or professional networks (such as LinkedIn) are governed by the privacy policies of their respective platforms.
