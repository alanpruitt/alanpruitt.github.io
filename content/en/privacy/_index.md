## Scope, Effective Date, and Covered Entities

* **Effective Date:** November 10, 2026
* **Entities Covered:** `alanpruitt.com` (Educational Manifesto & Essay Publishing), `webcognita.com` (Webcognita LLC Consulting & Digital Assets), and companion client-side educational utilities (including `exwtoolkit.com`).

---

### 1. Overview and Commitment to Privacy

Webcognita LLC (a Veteran-Owned Small Business) and Alan Pruitt ("we," "our," or "us") respect your digital privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the minimal data collection practices associated with our web properties. We operate under strict data minimalism, transparency, and the principles of Curriculum-as-Code. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize visitor data, nor do we use visitor communications or client records to train public foundational AI models.

* **Institutional Separation Notice:** `alanpruitt.com` and `webcognita.com` are operated independently by Alan Pruitt and Webcognita LLC. These platforms do not collect, process, or store student educational records (FERPA) or protected health information (HIPAA) on behalf of Arizona Western College or any partner institution.

---

### 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential data required to deliver static content and fulfill professional consulting or educational transactions:

* **Passive Technical Telemetry:** When you visit `alanpruitt.com` or `webcognita.com`, standard server logs processed via our static hosting and edge delivery infrastructure (including GitHub Pages and Cloudflare DNS/TLS routing) may automatically record basic connection data, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, and access timestamps. This data is used solely for network security, routing, and uptime maintenance.
* **Active User Submissions:** If you contact us directly via email or purchase professional services or digital products via Webcognita LLC, we collect the necessary contact details (such as your name, institutional affiliation, and email address) solely to respond to your inquiries or fulfill your order.
* **Zero-PII Client-Side Simulators:** Interactive calculators and educational simulators (such as the ADA Title II ROI Calculator on `alanpruitt.com` and clinical teaching tools on `exwtoolkit.com`) execute entirely within your local browser memory. No user inputs, simulated vitals, or institutional financial models are transmitted to or stored on our servers.

---

### 3. Payment Processing via Stripe (`webcognita.com`)

All financial transactions for Webcognita LLC services (such as workshops, digital tool kits, or consulting engagements) are processed securely through **Stripe, Inc.**

* **Zero Server Storage:** Credit card numbers, banking details, and sensitive financial credentials **never touch our web servers**.
* **Compliance:** All payment flows are handled entirely within secure, tokenized Stripe checkout environments adhering to strict PCI-DSS security standards. We retain only transaction records necessary for tax accounting and corporate compliance.

---

### 4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

* **No Behavioral Tracking:** We do not use third-party advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or cross-site behavioral profiling scripts on our domains.
* **Local Storage & Session Tokens:** Interactive components or client-side utilities may use standard browser `localStorage` strictly to preserve your user interface preferences (such as English/Spanish language toggles, theme contrast settings, or temporary calculator states). This data remains exclusively on your local device.

---

### 5. Third-Party Links, Syndication, and Embedded Assets

Our sites feature links to external repositories (GitHub), newsletter syndication (`alanpruitt.substack.com`), scholarly OER Commons resources, professional networks (LinkedIn), or embedded demonstrations. If you subscribe to newsletter dispatches via Substack, your email subscription is managed directly under Substack's platform privacy policy and opt-out controls. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of any external site you visit.

---

### 6. Data Security and Retention

We implement industry-standard security protocols, including enforced HTTPS/TLS encryption across all domains, hardware-backed authentication keys for administrative access, and static hosting infrastructures that minimize attack surfaces. Contact inquiries and transaction records are retained only as long as necessary for legitimate business operations or legal tax obligations.
