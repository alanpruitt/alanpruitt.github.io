Voluntary federal workforce & community college guidance (issued February 13, 2026) operationalized across the entire AWC EXW Curriculum-as-Code fleet.

Voluntary Federal Framework • 5 + 7

While higher education is still debating generic syllabus disclaimers, the Arizona Western College Exercise and Wellness fleet (EXW 101, 150, 210, 245, and 265) proactively adopts U.S. Department of Labor (DOL) Employment and Training Administration (ETA) Training and Employment Notice (TEN) No. 07-25 (Artificial Intelligence Literacy Framework, issued February 13, 2026). Rather than waiting for mandates, we operationalize this voluntary federal workforce and community college blueprint—mapping all 5 Foundational Content Areas and 7 Community College Delivery Design Principles directly into Simon Sinek’s Golden Circle (WHY → HOW → WHAT) and our four-part clinical submission pipeline.

01 / 5 FOUNDATIONAL AREAS TEN 07-25 Part A

What Students Learn About AI

  • 1. Understand AI Principles: Probabilistic next-token prediction vs. deterministic federal SSoT rules; clinical hallucinations.
  • 2. Explore AI Uses: Case synthesis, bilingual patient handouts (EN/ES), and exercise/nutrition progressions.
  • 3. Direct AI Effectively: P-T-C-F prompt engineering and triple-hash (###) clinical case delimiter encapsulation.
  • 4. Evaluate Outputs: 150-word Clinical SSoT Audit verifying AI output against PAGA 2018, DGA 2025–2030, ACSM 12th Ed., and NASM OPT.
  • 5. Use AI Responsibly: Zero-PII FERPA/HIPAA hygiene, Traffic Light AI Policy, and mandatory AI synthesis disclosure.
02 / 7 DELIVERY PRINCIPLES TEN 07-25 Part B

How Community Colleges Deliver It

  • 1. Enable Experiential Learning: Hands-on Four-Part Submission Standard and live exwtoolkit.com simulation in every module.
  • 2. Embed Learning in Context: Authentic Yuma / Imperial Valley heat-stress (>110°F) kinesiology and sports nutrition workflows.
  • 3. Build Complementary Human Skills: Student as Architect (Pattern / Rule / Solve) + 150-word Active Verification Defense.
  • 4. Address Prerequisites & Equitable Access: $0 ZTC OER, zero-cost AI tools, mobile-first low-bandwidth text, EN/ES parity, and WCAG 2.2 AA (9:1).
  • 5–7. Pathways, Enabling Roles & Agility: 16-module cloud repository, practitioner-faculty role modeling, and Git-backed Curriculum-as-Code agility.
03 / GOLDEN CIRCLE + OER WHY → HOW → WHAT

Inside-Out Operational Execution

Every module across the 10-course EXW fleet sequences the voluntary federal framework from the inside out:

  • THE WHY (Purpose & Equity): DOL Principle 4 ($0 ZTC, WCAG 2.2 AA 9:1, EN/ES parity) + DOL Areas 1 & 5 (AI Principles & Responsible Use).
  • THE HOW (Method & Audit): DOL Principles 2, 3 & 7 + DOL Areas 3 & 4 (Pattern / Rule / Solve, P-T-C-F, SSoT Clinical Audit).
  • THE WHAT (Proof & Portfolio): DOL Principles 1, 5 & 6 + DOL Area 2 (256 10/10 Exemplar Solves, exwtoolkit.com tokens, and Capstone Portfolio).
