This audit verifies the provided syllabus content against the three specified criteria.

Overall, the syllabus is well-structured, clear, and highly scannable. It successfully meets the formatting and hierarchy requirements. However, it is missing critical administrative components necessary for a complete and rigorous syllabus.

---

## Audit Findings

### 1. Heading Hierarchies (Strictly h2)
**Status: PASS**

The heading hierarchy correctly uses `##` for all major sections. This adheres to the instruction to reserve the highest level (H1) for the Canvas title, ensuring a clean, logical structure for the document.

### 2. Administrative Policies and Expectations (Clarity and Rigor)
**Status: NEEDS IMPROVEMENT (Missing Content)**

While the existing content (Course Information, Description, Outcomes) is clear and rigorous, the syllabus is currently missing essential administrative policies and expectations. For an EXW150 level course, these elements are crucial for setting student expectations and ensuring academic integrity.

**Recommendations for Improvement:**

*   **Add a dedicated section for Grading Policies:** Clearly define how assignments, exams, participation, and projects are weighted.
*   **Add Academic Integrity Statement:** Include a clear policy regarding plagiarism, cheating, and academic honesty.
*   **Add Attendance/Participation Policy:** Define expectations for class attendance and participation.
*   **Add Late Work/Revision Policy:** Establish clear rules for submitting assignments past deadlines.
*   **Add Communication Expectations:** Specify preferred methods for student-instructor communication (e.g., email response times).

### 3. Formatting and Cognitive Load (Scannability)
**Status: PASS**

The formatting effectively leverages structure to minimize cognitive overload.

**Strengths:**

*   **Clear Segmentation:** The use of distinct H2 headings (`Course Information`, `Learning Outcomes`, `Required Textbook`) clearly separates different types of information.
*   **Scannable Lists:** The use of bullet points (implied in the list format) and numbered lists (for Learning Outcomes) makes the content easy to digest.
*   **Effective Use of Tables:** The Course Schedule is presented in a clear, structured table format, which is the most effective way to present weekly information.
*   **Bolding:** Key information (like the Course Number and Instructor) is appropriately highlighted.

The syllabus provides an excellent foundation. To elevate it to a complete, high-rigor document, the following actions are recommended:
