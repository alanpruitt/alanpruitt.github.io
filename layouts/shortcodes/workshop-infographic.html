1. The Secure Workflow

  • Authenticate via NetID: Use only enterprise-licensed tools like Google Gemini Enterprise to prevent public data training.
  • Use Structured Prompt Delimiters: Wrap context and tasks in triple hashes (###) to minimize model hallucinations.
  • Automate High-Yield Tasks: Use AI for syllabus compliance auditing, transcript synthesis, and rubric scaling.

2. Institutional Guardrails

  • Sanitize Data for FERPA Compliance: Manually scrub all student names, IDs, and PII before entering text into prompts.
  • Route Regulated Data Correctly: Direct all HIPAA or clinical workflows exclusively to U of A Soteria.
  • Maintain Human Authority: Faculty and staff retain final responsibility for all AI-generated pedagogical and administrative artifacts.

3. Tool Selection Guide

  • Google Gemini Enterprise / U of A GenAI: Internal administrative data, syllabus auditing, drafting, meeting summaries.
  • U of A Soteria: HIPAA / Regulated clinical workflows and protected health data.
  • Consumer AI (Unauthenticated): Public information only. Strictly prohibited for sensitive institutional or student data.
