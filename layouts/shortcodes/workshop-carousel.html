Security Boundary Slide 03
❌ Unauthenticated Consumer AI
  • Prompts & documents ingested for public model training.
  • No enterprise data confidentiality agreements.
  • Direct violation of institutional compliance guidelines.
✅ NetID Enterprise Portals
  • Google Gemini Enterprise, U of A GenAI, Copilot.
  • Zero-Training Environment: Internal data is protected.
  • Strictly walled sandbox for instructional drafting.

Rule: Never paste university data into unauthenticated consumer chatbots.

Compliance Guardrail Slide 07

The Human Sanitization Protocol

❌ Strip all Personally Identifiable Information (PII) before input:

• Student full names, NetIDs, Student IDs, email addresses.
• Identifiable personal anecdotes or unique medical/clinical details.

✅ Safe Context: Sanitized course outlines, prompts, generic rubrics.

⚠️ FERPA Warning: Stored student outputs can become official records. Maintain ephemeral chat sessions.

Executive Quick-Reference Slide 12

### U OF A GENAI OPERATIONAL RULES ###

  1. Enterprise Auth: Always log in using NetID into approved enterprise tools.
  2. De-Identify Context: Strip all student names, IDs, and PII before generating feedback.
  3. Structured Delimiters: Wrap reference context inside ### blocks.
  4. Route Regulated Data: Route HIPAA data strictly to Soteria; never input restricted data.
  5. Human Authority: AI drafts; you retain 100% final pedagogical responsibility.

Reclaim hours of administrative time while safeguarding institutional integrity.
